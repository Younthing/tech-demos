## Palette

Olive for focus (`#31402b`), gold for a short rest (`#5c471f`), violet for a long rest (`#332b4e`). Paper ink on the mode fields. The original hues recorded for the sweep are `#ba4949`, `#38858a`, `#397097`, and the measured page field `#af4949`. Contrast: `replica/design/contrast.txt`, 19 pairs, 0 failing AA.

## Logo brief

A leaf and the word Ripen. The leaf is a simple stem and one blade, not a tomato and not a clock face. It has to read at 16px and as a 32px favicon. Delivered here: `public/favicon.svg` and the leaf icon in the header. Do not share the original's red disc or check-shaped fruit.

## Voice

Calm, specific, brief. Calm does not mean vague. Specific does not mean a manual. Brief does not mean cute.

Do say what the control does. Don't echo the original's slogans.
Do use "focus", "short rest", "long rest". Don't use their product name in the timer.
Do tell the person when storage fails. Don't invent a cheer.
Do show the finish time as a clock time. Don't promise a streak.
Do keep the study pages factual. Don't present other people's comments as praise for Ripen.

Strings in the timer: "Begin", "Pause", "Resume", "New task", "The queue is empty. Add the next thing you actually intend to finish.", "No task selected", "Wraps up around …", "This browser refused to store the session.", "Focus block ended. Step away before the next one."
